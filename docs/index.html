Features

Everything you need to buy at the right price

A lightweight browser extension that does one thing well: track prices and tell you when to buy.

Track any product on any website

Works with Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, eBay, and thousands of other online stores. If it has a price, PriceWatch can track it.

Automatic price checks

Set your preferred check interval — hourly, daily, or weekly. PriceWatch runs silently in the background and checks prices on schedule.

Price drop notifications

Get a Chrome notification the moment a product falls below your target price. No email, no app — just a quiet alert when it matters.

Price history chart

See how a price has changed over time so you can judge whether today's deal is actually a deal, or just the everyday price.

Multiple sources per product

Add the same product from several retailers. PriceWatch tracks them all and shows you which seller currently has the lowest price.

Private by design

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. Nothing is ever sent to an external server.

How it works

Up and running in under a minute

No accounts, no configuration files, no setup wizards.

1

Install the extension

Add PriceWatch to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. It takes about five seconds and requires no sign-up.

2

Browse to a product page

Navigate to any product you want to track on Amazon or another supported store. Click the PriceWatch icon in your toolbar.

3

Set your target price

Enter the price you want to pay. PriceWatch saves the product and starts monitoring automatically.

4

Get notified when the price drops

When the price falls to or below your target, Chrome delivers a notification. Click it to go straight to the product page.
